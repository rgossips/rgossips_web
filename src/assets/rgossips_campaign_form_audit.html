Campaign form audit with optimizations and new field suggestions

Optimize existing field Add new field High Priority Medium Priority
Section 1 — Basic info (optimizations)
Campaign type → controls field visibility
When brand selects "Barter", hide Budget (Total) and Budget/Influencer. Show a new field "Product value (approx.)" instead. When "Paid" is selected, show budget fields. When "Hybrid" is selected, show both product value and budget fields. This prevents confusion — right now a barter campaign shows ₹50,000 which misleads influencers.
AutomationHigh
Budget/Influencer → auto-calculate
Don't let brands type this manually. Auto-compute: Budget (Total) / Slots = Budget per Influencer. Show it as read-only with a grey background. Prevents mismatches where total says ₹50K but per-influencer says ₹10K.
AutomationHigh
Description → add structured placeholder
Replace generic "Describe goals..." with a guided template: "What is this campaign about? / What do you want the influencer to highlight? / Any specific messaging or hashtags?" This produces better briefs. Many brands leave the description vague which wastes influencer time.
UX improvementMedium
Section 2 — Media & deliverables (optimizations)
Content deliverables → add total count indicator
Show a live counter below: "Total deliverables: 5 pieces". Also, make at least 1 deliverable required before publishing — right now a brand can publish with all zeros, which makes no sense.
ValidationMedium
Content deliverables → add "Blogs" option
You have Reels, Posts, Stories, Videos — but missing Blog Posts. Some brands need written content for SEO. Add a fifth counter for "Blogs" and optionally "Tweets/X posts" for Twitter campaigns.
Missing optionMedium
Section 3 — Influencer requirements (optimizations)
Location → change from free-text to multi-select dropdown
Right now it's a comma-separated text field. This will cause data nightmares: "Mumbai" vs "mumbai" vs "Bombay". Replace with a searchable multi-select dropdown pulling from your cities master table. Also add an "All India" / "Pan India" toggle at the top that auto-selects all.
Data qualityHigh
Categories → make selectable (toggle chips)
The chips currently look like static labels. Make them toggleable — tap to select (fills with purple), tap again to deselect. Show selected count: "3 of 15 selected". Also require at least 1 category to be selected before publishing.
UX improvementHigh
Influencer Tier + Min/Max Followers → auto-sync
When "Micro" tier is selected, auto-fill Min: 10K, Max: 100K. When "Nano" is selected, auto-fill Min: 1K, Max: 10K. The brand can still override, but it saves time and prevents illogical combos like selecting "Nano" tier but setting Min Followers to 500K.
AutomationMedium
Section 4 — Schedule (optimizations)
Split "Deadline" into 2 separate dates
Your current note says "applications close and campaign ends on this date" — but these are usually different. Split into: (1) Application deadline — when influencers can stop applying, (2) Campaign end date — when all content must be delivered. A campaign might stop accepting applications on May 15 but content delivery runs until June 15.
Critical fixHigh
New fields — Brand & product context
Platform(s) *
Multi-select: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Blog. Determines which social handles the influencer needs. A brand might want Reels on Instagram AND Shorts on YouTube from the same campaign.
New fieldHigh
Product / service to be promoted
Short text field: "What exactly are you promoting?" e.g., "Moisturizing cream - 50ml tube" or "Weekend stay at our Mussoorie resort." This is the single most important thing an influencer needs to know before applying. Currently missing entirely.
New fieldHigh
Will product be shipped to influencer?
Toggle: Yes / No / Pickup required. If "Yes", show sub-field: "Shipping timeline (days)". This sets clear expectations — influencers need to know if they'll receive the product before content creation deadline.
New fieldHigh
Compensation details (for barter)
For barter campaigns: describe what the influencer gets. e.g., "Free 2-night stay + meals" or "Full skincare kit worth ₹3,500". Influencers reject vague barter offers — being specific increases application rate.
New fieldMedium
New fields — Content guidelines
Do's and don'ts
Two text areas: "Must include" (e.g., "Show the product packaging, mention the discount code") and "Must avoid" (e.g., "Don't show competitor products, don't use background music with copyright"). This eliminates 80% of revision cycles.
New fieldHigh
Hashtags to use
Text field with auto-# prefix. e.g., "#RGossips #SkincareThatWorks #Ad". Pre-populate #Ad or #Paidpartnership for ASCI compliance (mandatory in India for paid collaborations).
New fieldHigh
Brand handles to tag
Auto-filled from brand profile's Instagram/YouTube handle. Brand just confirms. e.g., "@rgossips_official". Saves time and ensures correct handle is tagged every time.
New fieldHigh
Reference content / mood board
Upload area for 2-3 reference images or video links (Instagram/YouTube URLs). "Show influencers the style you're looking for." Brands that share references get 3x better first drafts from creators.
New fieldMedium
Content approval required?
Toggle: Yes (draft review before posting) / No (influencer posts directly). If Yes, show: "Review turnaround time: 24hrs / 48hrs / 72hrs". Sets clear expectations on both sides.
New fieldMedium
New fields — Influencer targeting
Preferred gender
Multi-select: Male / Female / Any. A men's grooming brand only wants male creators. A bridal jewellery brand wants female creators. Currently no way to specify this.
New fieldHigh
Preferred language(s)
Multi-select from languages master: Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Kannada, Bengali, etc. Critical for India where regional content drives higher engagement in Tier-2/3 cities.
New fieldHigh
Target audience age group
Multi-select: 13-17, 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45+. Helps match with influencers whose audience demographics align. e.g., An EdTech brand targeting 18-24 shouldn't get matched with a parenting influencer whose audience is 30-40.
New fieldMedium
New fields — Terms & logistics
Content usage rights
Dropdown: "Influencer's page only" / "Brand can repost" / "Brand can use in paid ads" / "Full rights transfer". This is legally critical and most platforms ignore it. Influencers charge more for ad usage rights — being upfront prevents disputes.
New fieldHigh
Content keep-up duration
How long must the post stay live? Dropdown: 24 hours (stories) / 7 days / 30 days / Permanent. Some brands want permanent posts, others just need a 24hr story. Influencers need this clarity upfront.
New fieldMedium
Exclusivity clause
Toggle: Yes / No. If Yes: "Influencer cannot promote competing brands for ___ days" (dropdown: 7 / 15 / 30 / 60 / 90 days). A skincare brand paying ₹20K doesn't want the influencer promoting a competitor the next day.
New fieldMedium
Payment timeline
For paid campaigns: "When will the influencer be paid?" Dropdown: Advance / On content approval / Within 7 days of posting / Within 30 days. Biggest pain point for influencers — setting this upfront builds trust on your platform.
New fieldMedium
